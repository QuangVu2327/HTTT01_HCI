###### **Interaction Design** 



- Not only concerned with _designing products_ 

- But also with _designing the ways people interact with & use those products_ in real world 

- Improve the UX 

   - Not always necessary to design new features 

   - Proposing a _different way of using the existing product_ (or redesigning the workflow around it) 

5 

HCI - FIT - HCMUS 

###### **Design** 



- An intervention in an existing situation to bring improvement, rather than a new product creation 

   - A product, an interface, a process, a way of use, a new method of organizing work 

- Ultimate goal 

   - Transform an unsatisfactory current situation into a better one 

6 

HCI - FIT - HCMUS 



#### **“** 

_The process of creating a solution to achieve a desired_ **_goals_** _within specific real-world_ **_constraints and conditions_** 

###### **Design** 



- Goal 

   - What is design purpose we plan to develop? 

   - Who is the intended user or target audience of this design? 

   - Why would they need or desire this product? 

- Constraint 

   - What _materials_ will be required, and what _standards_ must be met? 

   - What is the target _cost_ of the product? 

   - How much _time_ will be required to develop the product? 

   - Are there any health and safety _issues_ that need to be considered? 

8 

HCI - FIT - HCMUS 

###### **Example** 



- Design a wireless personal movie-viewing device 

- Goals 

   - Affluent young users who watch movies while commuting, download free versions, and share their experiences with friends 

- Constraints 

   - Is it rainproof? 

   - Do we have to use existing video standards to download movies? 

   - Do we need to integrate copyright protection? 

9 

HCI - FIT - HCMUS 

###### **Design** 



- Not all goals can be met with multiple constraints 

- Important : the balance/the trade-off – Decide what to relax so the rest is achievable 

- Skill 

   - Accepting conflicting constraints 

   - Pick the right compromise 

10 

HCI - FIT - HCMUS 

###### **Example** 



- Design a wireless personal movie-viewing device 

- Trade-off 

   - Free versions/share vs. copyright 

   - Rainproof vs. ultra-thin design 

   - On-the-go visual quality vs. portability & battery life 

   - Keep: premium mobile viewing + social sharing 

   - Give up: free downloads and built-in ultra-durable waterproofing

###### **Discussion** 



- Human nature 

   - Often leads to mistakes, errors, and omissions 

- Therefore, a system should be designed to – Reduce the likelihood of errors occurring 

- – Minimize the consequences when errors occur 

- Understand know how human error occurs
