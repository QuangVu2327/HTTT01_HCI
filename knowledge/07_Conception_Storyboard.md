1. UI & UX 

#### User Experience (UX) 



- How users perceive and experience a product 

- Goal 

   - Satisfy users' needs by making the product effective, efficient, and enjoyable to use 

   - – Eg. 

      - A pair of scissors: cutting should be easy and efficient 

      - A park bench: sitting on it should be as comfortable as possible

##### Usability vs. User Exoerience 

Usability: how a person performsa task. User Experience: how a person feels.

#### User Interface (UI) 



- The part of a product that users interact with – Eg. 

   - A pair of scissors: the handles 

   - A park bench: the seat and backrest 

###### • Goal 

- Enable intuitive and efficient interaction with the product

#### Context 



- Consists of 

   - Users 

   - Tasks 

   - Equipment 

   - Physical environment 

– Social environment 

- Temporal & Emotional context 

20 

HCI - FIT - HCMUS 

#### Scenario + Context 



- Scenario 

   - Explains what the user does, what happens 

- Context of use 

   - The set of circumstances surrounding a user's interaction with a product 

   - The combination of users, tasks, equipment, and the physical and social environments in which a product is used 

21 

HCI - FIT - HCMUS 

#### Example 



###### • Scenario + context 

Dr. Lan is <mark>working in a busy emergency department</mark> during the morning rush. She finishes entering a patient's clinical notes and prescribes routine medication. Throughout the documentation process, <mark>the Save button remains visible at the bottom of the</mark> screen. After clicking Save, the system immediately highlights a required field that has been left blank and displays a clear message explaining what information is missing. Dr. Lan corrects the field within seconds and successfully submits the record. She quickly moves on to the next patient, <mark>confident that the documentation is complete and accurate.</mark>

HCI - FIT - HCMUS 

#### Example 



###### • Scenario + context 

|**Context element**|**Scenario + Context**|
|---|---|
|User|Dr. Lan, an emergency physician|
|Task|Enter clinical notes, prescribe medication,<br>submit the record|
|Physical environment|Busy emergency department|
|Temporal Context|During the morning rush|
|Social environment|Other patients and healthcare staff are nearby|
|Equipment|Hospital information system on a workstation|
|Emotional Context|Patient has severe chest pain; workload is high|

HCI - FIT - HCMUS 

#### Example 



###### • Design decisions are driven by the context of use rather than the task 

|**Context**|**Immediate Design Decisions**|
|---|---|
|Busy emergency department|Reduce cognitive load, simplify the interface|
|Morning rush|Minimize clicks, support faster workflows|
|Documentation task|Keep Save always visible, provide auto-save|
|High risk of errors|Highlight invalid fields, provide actionable error messages|
|Time pressure|Show only critical information first|
|Need for confidence|Confirm successful submission and completion|
