### Scenario 



- An informal **_narrative description_** of how a user performs a task and achieves a goal in a specific context 

- Note 

   - Focus on the user's activities rather than the software or technology used to accomplish the task 

   - Use the users' own words and terminology to ensure the scenario is easily understood by all stakeholders 

   - A communication tool throughout the product development process 

10 

HCI - FIT - HCMUS 

### Why use scenarios? 



- To focus on **_users' activities_** rather than the system or technology 

   - Why users perform their activities 

   - Goals that users are trying to achieve 

- Scenarios + Persona 

   - Place a specific user in a realistic context 

   - Understand how users perform tasks to achieve goals 

   - Keep the design process focused on real users and their needs 

   - Provide a realistic basis for evaluating design solutions and user experiences

### Example 3 



Dr. Lan finishes entering a patient's clinical notes and prescribes routine medication. Before submitting the record, she scrolls through the page to review the information but cannot immediately find the Save button. After finally saving, the system displays the message "Validation failed." without indicating which field is incorrect. She spends several minutes searching for the missing information while the patient waits.

HCI - FIT - HCMUS 

### Example 4 



Dr. Lan is working in a busy emergency department during the morning rush. She finishes entering a patient's clinical notes and prescribes routine medication. Throughout the documentation process, the Save button remains visible at the bottom of the screen. After clicking Save, the system immediately highlights a required field that has been left blank and displays a clear message explaining what information is missing. Dr. Lan corrects the field within seconds and successfully submits the record. She quickly moves on to the next patient, confident that the documentation is complete and accurate.

### Example 5 



_Say I want to find a movie directed by Martin Scorses. I don’t remember the title but I know it came out in the cinemas around 2006 or 2007. I go to the club website and choose the director option. A huge list of directors is displayed – I had no idea there were so many directors with surnames beginning with S. After scrolling through the list I find Martin Scorses and choose to see further details about him. Another long list of movies eventually leads me to the movie I was looking for – The Departed. As an existing club member, I need to enter my username and password to be able to rent the movie. Once my password has been confirmed, I am given a choice of rental period and payment method. I have my preferences already registered in the system, so I just choose the defaults and download my movie._
