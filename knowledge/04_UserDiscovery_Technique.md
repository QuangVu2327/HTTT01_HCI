## Discussion 



- Let's suppose we ask "What do you want?” 

- • Do you still remember Steve Jobs' viewpoint? 

- Answers might be 

   - Customer : “Something inexpensive” 

   - End user : “Something that makes me happy” 

- Have to balance both sides 

   - Customer : it was worth, and decided to buy 

   - – End user : it’s useful, and use regularly

_Designing features and UIs, prioritize understanding the_ **_end-users_** _' tasks, challenges, and goals. Then ensure that the product also satisfies the_ **_customer_** _'s business requirements._ 

## Example 



- A hospital is preparing to purchase Outpatient Management Software 

- Customer: Hospital Director 

   - Decide whether or not to buy the software 

- Influencer: Head of the Outpatient Department – Don't use the software for daily patient examinations, but have a significant role in selection and deployment 

- • End-users: Doctors and Nurses 

   - Use the software to register patients, view records, prescribe medication, etc.

## Influencer 



- Case 1: Software proposal 

- Hospital Director 

   - "Should we buy software A or software B?” 

- Head of the Outpatient Department 

   - "I've looked at both. _Software A is more suitable_ for the department's examination process" 

- Understands the work of doctors and nurses 

- Not the one paying, influence the hospital to choose A over B

## Influencer 



- Case 2: Training and encouraging use 

- Doctor complains 

   - "This software is difficult to use" 

- Head of the Outpatient Department 

   - "We'll continue using it. I'll organize a training session and send feedback to the vendor for improvement" 

- "This software slows down the examination process. I'll suggest the hospital switch to a different solution” 

- • Influence the level of acceptance and usage by end users

## Discussion 



- Ethnography 

   - _How do the context,_ workflow _and environment shape users' behavior?_ 

- Shadowing 

   - _How does this user perform the task?_ 

- Think aloud 

   - _What is the user thinking while performing the task?_

   - "What around the doctor is influencing the doctor's behavior?" 

- Shadowing looks at the doctor's hands 

   - "What is the doctor doing?” 

- Think-aloud listens inside the doctor’s mind 

   - "What is the doctor thinking while performing the task?"
