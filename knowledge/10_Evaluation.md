## Usability Test 



###### _“ ”_ • How do users use the product? 

###### • Goal 

- Check whether users can **_use the product to complete tasks_** effectively and easily 

- **Effectiveness** : Can users complete the task? 

- **Efficiency** : How quickly and easily can they complete the task? 

- **Errors** : What errors do they make? 

- **Learnability** : How easy is it for them to learn how to use the product? 

- **Satisfaction** : Do they feel comfortable and satisfied when using it?
