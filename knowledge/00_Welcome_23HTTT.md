## **Objective** 



- (1) State and interpret **_concepts_** of the HCI design field 

   - Human/machine factors, interaction models, user behaviors, and the design process 

      - Especially, interactions with people using Information System 

- (2) Apply HCI **_principles_** & **_interaction design process_** to the interface design or improvement for a particular IS 

- (3) Evaluate the **_usability & interactivity_** of interface designs

## **Objective** 



- (4) **_Implement interfaces/interactions_** in Information Systems by using UI design tools and programming languages 

   - Paper & video prototyping 

   - Coding 

- (5) Practice to **_give and receive feedbacks_** for design enhancement
